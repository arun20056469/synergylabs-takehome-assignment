# AuroraDesk product overview

AuroraDesk is a cloud workspace for support teams. The Starter plan includes 15 named users, a shared inbox, and 5 GB of attachment storage. The Growth plan includes 50 named users, automation rules, analytics dashboards, and 100 GB of attachment storage. The Enterprise plan adds unlimited named users, SAML single sign-on, and a dedicated success manager.

AuroraDesk retains deleted tickets for 30 days before permanent deletion. The service status page is updated during incidents and planned maintenance.
