# Data retention policy

Workspace audit events are stored for 365 days on the Growth and Enterprise plans and for 90 days on the Starter plan. Ticket attachments are included in each plan's storage allowance. When a workspace is closed, administrators have 30 days to export its content before the workspace is permanently removed.

Data export files are available for download for 14 days. The privacy portal is the preferred route for individual data-access requests.
